## Human-Aware test

To see investigation coverage change naturally:

- inspect two files thoroughly,
- revisit one of them,
- verify integrity,
- triage all files,
- leave one high-significance artifact with comparatively little focused review.

Do not expect a fixed blind-spot percentage. Coverage is based on the reviewer’s actual recorded interaction.
